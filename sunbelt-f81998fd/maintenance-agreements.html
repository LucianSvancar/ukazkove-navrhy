Maintenance agreements: save money and get priority service

Preventative maintenance agreements are one of the best money savers we offer.

Call (405) 273-5772Request a Quote

We do all the work for you

Save money, and receive priority on any service calls the rest of the year.

For residential customers, we schedule all of your preventative maintenance for you at a fixed price. We keep your equipment running in tip top shape, so it lasts longer. It is easy to forget to check the furnace filter, but with a maintenance agreement from Sunbelt, we do all the work for you.

You also get 10% off your labor charges if you have to have us out at any other time to repair your system. You probably will not need us, because before the extreme demands hit your heating and cooling equipment, we will already have been to your home and cleaned and checked your system.

It is a great service for any homeowner or landlord. For commercial customers, call us and we will quote an agreement to meet your needs.
