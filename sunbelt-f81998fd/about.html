Comfort is more than a temperature

Today, indoor air quality covers a lot more than the number on the thermostat.

A few years ago we looked at the thermostat to see whether the space was in the comfort range, and that was all we needed. Now we know that gases and particulates can build up in a tight home and cause health issues. Humidity matters too: high indoor humidity can contribute to mold and odors that affect indoor air quality.

Unfortunately, many homes in the Shawnee area, even new ones, have poorly designed and installed comfort systems. Buying a new home is no guarantee that you will not end up with major problems in the heating and cooling system.

That is why we offer advanced services such as infiltration testing (blower door test), duct leakage testing, infrared inspection, Low-E glass testing and airflow test and balance. Combined with high-efficiency heating and cooling products from Lennox, WaterFurnace and Fujitsu, they let us give our customers an extremely low cost of operation.
