Blower Door Infiltration Testing

Good insulation is commonly mistaken for all that is needed to make a building envelope perform. Some insulating methods do offer better protection against moving air, but insulation by itself does not guarantee the desired performance.

A blower door test is quite simple. A technician installs a big calibrated fan in an outside opening of the structure, and the amount of air that leaks from the structure is measured at different pressures. The numbers let us estimate how the structure will perform in the real world.

There can be good news and bad news. Many construction issues that make a building leak are hard to resolve after it is built, which is the bad news. The good news is that in most cases a loose structure can be improved by identifying the issue and sealing it. Best results come when the trades understand the methods for a tight envelope and work toward the goal of building a tight structure.

We have been doing blower door testing in the Pottawatomie County area since the mid 1980s. Few companies in the entire state have been proving their performance longer than we have. This article was written by Dean Akin, President and Founder of Sunbelt.
