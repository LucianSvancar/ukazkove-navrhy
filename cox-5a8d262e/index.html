Welcome

We treat you like a neighbor, because you are one.

We are Cox Creek Family Dentistry, a general dental practice at 721 Cox Creek Parkway in Florence. We keep things simple: a friendly voice on the phone, a clear explanation in the chair, and care for adults and children alike.

Making you feel welcome matters more to us than anything on the wall. If you have not seen a dentist in a while, that is fine. Call us and we will start from where you are.

What we do

General dentistry, close to home.

  • Checkups and cleanings

    A regular visit is the easiest way to keep small problems small. We look, we clean, and we tell you plainly what we see.

  • Fillings and repairs

    Chipped, cracked or sore teeth are best looked at early. Call us and we will find a time to see you.

  • X-rays and exams

    Pictures help us see what the eye cannot, so we can explain your options before anything is done.

Children, a brighter smile, or a question about a treatment? Ask us and we will talk it through.
