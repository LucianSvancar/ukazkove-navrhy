Get Ready for Spring With a Working Air Conditioner

April 5, 2021

Sometimes it’s the little things that get to us. Whether it’s spilling coffee or tripping over a crack in the sidewalk, it’s always something unexpected. Now that spring has arrived, ignoring an unexpected problem with your air conditioner can often end up costing you more in the long run. That’s why you should make sure your air conditioner is working properly so life’s surprises don’t cause you to feel the heat.

Read on to learn more about why you should check if your air conditioner is working and how Goff HVAC & Plumbing can help.

Why You Should Make Sure Your AC Is Working

Now that spring is upon us, warm weather is just around the corner. Right now is the perfect time to inspect your air conditioner, especially in March and April before the hotter weather kicks in.

The benefits of a properly functioning air conditioner include lower energy bills, improved air quality, better sleeping conditions, and a sense of peace knowing that your system is performing at its absolute best.

Signs Your AC Isn’t Working

Symptoms of a broken air conditioner can leave you and your family sweating in the heat. Here are just a few signs it’s broken:

Warm air coming out of the vent

Not enough airflow

Unit frequently turns on and off

Leaking water

Weird or loud noises

Contact Us Today

If you suspect your air conditioner isn’t working properly or may need to be repaired, don’t hesitate to contact Goff HVAC & Plumbing today. Serving the Texarkana area, we can service all makes and models. Whether it’s a smaller unit or a larger project, our licensed technicians can handle it. That includes repairs, installations, and maintenance for your air conditioner.

We offer 24/7 emergency service if needed, to ensure your safety during extreme heat. If you ever need any equipment replaced, we offer free estimations so you never have to worry about the cost.

Even though life is full of surprises, a broken air conditioner shouldn’t be one of them. With spring in full bloom, now is the time to make sure the AC is working efficiently. If you suspect your air conditioner isn’t functioning properly, now is the time to do something about it. Don’t let life get you down when Goff HVAC & Plumbing can keep you comfortable.
