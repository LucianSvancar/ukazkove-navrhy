How Reme Halo Air Purifier Can Protect You and Your Family

March 3, 2021

In these unprecedented times, you and your family members may stay at home as much as possible to avoid contracting COVID-19. Unfortunately, you can’t predict when the virus will enter your home, and non-sick or elderly family members are at higher risk of complications. To protect your family, consider investing in a Reme Halo air purifier.

How Does the Reme Halo Kill Viruses?

The Reme Halo makes use of a higher Ionized Hydro-peroxide output with an enhanced Zinc catalyst, allowing faster kill rates for bacteria in the air and on surfaces. Its technologies reduce odors, air pollutants, VOCs (chemical odors), smoke, mold, bacteria, and viruses by generating an Advanced Oxidation Plasma consisting of Ionized Hydro-peroxides, Superoxide ions, and Hydroxide ions.

How Effective Is the Reme Halo?

The Reme Halo air purifier is so effective that it is used in hospitals around the world. Schools, cruise ships, military hospitals, and factories have also installed the Reme Halo in their HVAC systems. A third-party study conducted by Innovative Bionanalysis Laboratories in Cypress, California has even claimed that the Reme Halo is 99.9% effective against COVID-19!

Benefits of Clean Air

Though Coronavirus is a major concern, it will eventually pass. When it does, it’s important to remember how important clean air in your home is to your health. Think about the amount of time you spend in your home, and how often you deal with odors, allergies, dust, and dander. No amount of air filtration and purification can stop all these things.

Reme Halo is only one aspect of a comprehensive HVAC plan for controlling your air. Regularly cleaning and replacing your filters, utilizing whole-home humidifiers and dehumidifiers if needed, and maintaining clean ductwork are recommended to maintain home comfort and health.

How Do You Get a Reme Halo Air Purifier?

When you purchase a Reme Halo, you don’t have to invest in a brand-new heating and cooling system. The Reme Halo is compatible with most existing HVAC systems and the system doesn’t take up extra space.
