Quick, Compassionate Care for Hurting Smiles

For urgent issues such as a broken arm, patients know they can visit their nearest emergency room for care. But when the emergency involves dental health, many people aren’t sure where to begin. At Pleasant Grove Family Dentistry, we do our utmost to see patients experiencing a dental emergency right away. If you need help with toothache, jaw pain, or other urgent dental problems, call our Texarkana dentist and team right away. In many cases, we’ll be able to see you on the same day you contact us; if we can’t, we can still help you with directions for at-home care and pain management.

Why Choose Pleasant Grove Family Dentistry for Emergency Dentistry?

  • Same-Day Appointments Available
  • Down-to-Earth, High-Quality Dentistry
  • Experienced Dentists and Team

How We Treat Dental Emergencies

  • Same-Day Appointment: Step one is for you to call our office. Once we have confirmed that urgent care is necessary, we can schedule an appointment for you. Depending on your symptoms, our team may give you a few first-aid tips so that you can keep the situation under control until you can get to our office.
  • Emergency Exam: Your mouth will be examined closely, and X-rays will be taken as needed. Our goal will be to narrow down the true source of the problem so that it can be dealt with properly.
  • Review Findings: We’ll share the results of our examination with you and give you our treatment recommendations. When suggesting any sort of service, we’ll be sure to explain how long you can expect it to take as well as what the price will look like.
  • Quality Care for Your Smile: Finally, we’ll take whatever steps are necessary to get your oral health back on track.

The Most Common Dental Emergencies

You may be hesitant about reaching out to our practice for treatment if you’re not sure whether your dental issue counts as an emergency. It’s generally a good idea to reach out whenever you have doubts about your oral health; that way, you can get the care you need as soon as possible. Below are examples of urgent dental situations that we can help with.

Toothaches

Is My Toothache a Dental Emergency? Whether you recently started to notice that one of your teeth feels a bit sensitive or you’ve been dealing with a dull discomfort for a while now, we do recommend reaching out to our team at Pleasant Grove Family Dentistry. That way, we can learn more about your symptoms and determine if you’d benefit from an emergency exam.

How You Should Handle a Toothache: Until your appointment with us, we recommend avoiding anything that could exacerbate your symptoms, like eating spicy foods. You should also drink room-temperature water, place a cold compress against the outside of your cheek for 10 minutes at a time, and take OTC pain medication as directed, if needed.

How We Treat Toothaches: First, we need to complete a comprehensive oral exam to determine what’s causing your toothache. Then, we will review our findings, discuss your treatment options, and help you decide the best way to proceed. Before you know it, your healthy, pain-free smile will be restored.

Chipped Teeth

Is a Chipped Tooth a Dental Emergency? Not always. For example, if the chip is so minor that you aren’t experiencing dental sensitivity or any other abnormal symptoms, then you should be safe to schedule an appointment at our office before the end of the week instead of on the same day.

How You Should Handle a Chipped Tooth: Since the tooth is at risk of breaking further, it’s very important that you avoid crunchy foods, like hard pretzels. Instead, you should stick to soft foods, avoid chewing on that side of your mouth, and rinse with clean, room-temperature water periodically. You should also cover the sharp edge with dental wax or sugar-free gum to prevent your tongue, lips, or the insides of your cheeks from getting cut.

How We Treat Chipped Teeth: We offer a few solutions at our Texarkana dental office, including cosmetic bonding and veneers. During your consultation, we will share which one we recommend for you and answer any questions you have so you can confidently decide how you’d like to proceed.

Cracked Tooth

Is a Cracked Tooth a Dental Emergency? A cracked tooth is considered a dental emergency, even if the damage is relatively minor. After all, the more time that passes, the higher the chances are of it breaking. So, don’t wait – schedule an appointment with one of our emergency dentists in Texarkana right away.

How You Should Handle a Cracked Tooth: It’s very important that you don’t do anything that could cause the tooth to break further, like chewing on that side of your mouth. Instead, you should rinse your mouth periodically with clean, room-temperature water, stick to extremely soft foods, and take OTC pain medication as directed until your appointment.

How We Treat Cracked Teeth: Ideally, we’ll be able to restore the look, strength, and health of your tooth with a dental crown. If that’s not an option because the tooth is damaged beyond the point of repair, then we will perform an extraction and fill the newly open space with a dental bridge or implant.
