4 Pros Of Getting Straighter Teeth With Six Month Smiles in Texarkana

June 12, 2019

Call (430) 342-1083Request appointment

Every time you go out and meet new people, you can’t help but be self-conscious of your crooked and misaligned teeth. You just want to be able to have a conversation with someone new without your smile distracting them. Six Month Smiles in Texarkana is a clear braces system that is crafted to straighten your front visible teeth in as little as six months. They’re not as cumbersome or noticeable as traditional braces, so you won’t have to worry about compromising your professional image! Read on to learn 4 reasons Six Month Smiles can be the right option for you.

Get A Straightened Smile Faster

Treating your crooked and misaligned smile with traditional braces can take up to two years. If you’ve got an upcoming summer wedding or family reunion, you don’t have that kind of time. One of the biggest advantages of Six Month Smiles is that treatment typically takes about six months. It’s important to note that this treatment doesn’t take less time because it’s moving your teeth at a faster pace; it’s quicker because it’s only straightening your front teeth. The benefit of this is that you can feel confident when you smile for photos and while you’re having conversations because that’s the only portion of your mouth that others will see.

A Discreet Braces System

Six Month Smiles braces system is less noticeable and cumbersome than traditional metallic braces. They utilize tooth-colored wires and clear brackets that blend in with your mouth. If you’re an adult that never got the opportunity to get braces when you were in middle or high school, this is a great option for you! You can maintain a professional image while attaining a straighter smile.

Cost-Effective Treatment Option

Because this braces system can treat you in such a short time-span, it typically costs less than other orthodontic treatments. The cost usually reflects the length of your treatment, the price of the braces, and sometimes the skill or experience of your dental professional. Comparing the costs of different treatments and researching providers with experience and knowledge can ensure you receive the best quality of care.

Improve Your Oral Health

When you think about the benefits of getting a straighter smile, the first thing that makes you excited is probably that your self-confidence will boost. However, this isn’t the only benefit! By aligning your teeth, you can also improve your oral health by making your mouth easier to clean. Crooked teeth have countless nooks and crannies that are hard to clean food debris and plaque out of. When these build up, it can cause oral health issues like gum disease and dental decay. With your new straightened teeth, it’ll be easier to clean them because there are fewer places for bacteria and plaque to hide.

If you’re on a time crunch and want aligned teeth that make you proud to show off your smile, Six Month Smiles is the treatment for you. Visiting your dentist in Texarkana for a consultation can put you on the path for a seamless smile that beams self-assurance!
