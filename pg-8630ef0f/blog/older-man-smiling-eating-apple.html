Wondering How Dental Implants in Texarkana Feel? Here are the Details

April 10, 2019

Call (430) 342-1083Request appointment

Since losing some of your teeth, you’ve become aware of just how valuable they are because you’ve had to alter many of your normal activities, like chewing food, smiling and speaking. Although you’ve done your best to adjust, you’d like to regain the life you once had. You’ve heard about dental implants in Texarkana as an option, but you’re not familiar with how the process works and how they’ll feel once they’re placed. As you read on, a local implant dentist weighs in to provide the answers you need so you can get on with having your smile and confidence restored!

How Does a Dental Implant Work?

When a tooth is missing, the primary problem is that the root is gone, which binds the tooth to the underlying jawbone. A dental implant is the only tooth replacement process that addresses this issue head-on.

A titanium rod that is surgically inserted into the jawbone, the dental implant mimics the natural function of the tooth’s root. Because titanium is biocompatible, it is less likely to create adverse effects when the rod is placed. Thus, a natural process is initiated called osseointegration, in which the existing bone fuses with the implant to create a firm and lasting foundation.

How Does a Dental Implant Feel?

A dental implant is unlike other forms of tooth replacement that merely rest above the gums and rely on such factors as suction, dental adhesive or other teeth to act as abutments (anchors). With these options, patients are left with more artificial-feeling fixtures.

By being affixed to the jawbone, though, dental implants provide a natural sensation along with a more aesthetically-pleasing smile. Here are two other factors that play a role in recreating the original environment in your mouth:

  • Titanium Abutment – After your gums and underlying bone have sufficiently healed, a titanium abutment is screwed into the implant. This is an important step for securing the final restoration and creating a natural feel, because it acts as an anchor that holds the new tooth in place.
  • Porcelain Restoration – Once the abutment is in place, a porcelain restoration can be added. This is usually the most commonly used material because its color and hardness are quite similar to natural teeth.

One thing to keep in mind is that the porcelain fixture won’t have any sensation. However, you likely won’t notice any difference between your newly restored tooth and your others.

It’s Worth a Visit

To learn more about the dental implant process, your best option is to visit your dentist in Texarkana. While you’re there, you’ll undergo a thorough examination to make sure you’re a good candidate for the procedure, and the professional will provide all the information you need about the process.

Your path to a fully revamped smile starts by reaching out to schedule a visit today!
