Is a Root Canal in Texarkana Painful? Here’s What You Need to Know

October 17, 2018

Call (430) 342-1083Request appointment

The root canal in Texarkana is one of the most misunderstood forms of dental therapy available today. People often mistakenly associate root canals with pain and discomfort. In reality, modern root canal therapy is not only comfortable and gentle, it’s a great way to save a tooth that might otherwise be lost to extraction.

What Is a Root Canal Anyway?

Your teeth are made up of three layers. The outer surface is crafted from enamel, an exceptionally hard material which keeps the tooth healthy over many decades of use. The first inner layer is made from dentin, a strong, yellowish substance. At the heart of each tooth is the pulp, which contains nerves and blood vessels.

At the very bottom of the pulp, beneath the gums, is the root canal, a complex network of sensitive nerve endings. When a tooth becomes infected the invading bacteria work their way through each layer until they reach the root canal itself. If it’s lost, then the remaining option may be to extract what’s left of the tooth and leave an empty socket behind.

Fortunately your dentist has a proven way to stop the decay process in its tracks. Root canal is the name given to this form of therapy. Here’s how it works:

  1. Your dentist will explain the procedure, answer any questions and administer pain blockers to prevent you from feeling any discomfort. He or she may also use a mild form of anesthesia to help you relax.
  2. The dentist will remove the decayed portion of the tooth down to the root canal. This is important for preventing further infection.
  3. After the harmful bacteria is eliminated the dentist will fill the cavity.
  4. In most cases the patient will return for a follow-up visit in which the dentist applies a crown or other restoration. This protects the tooth over the long run and restore lost functionality.

Recovery from a root canal only takes a few days. Soon you’ll be feeling great and ready to get on with your life.

Why a Root Canal Beats an Extraction

When you’re suffering from a nasty toothache an extraction may seem like the only course of action. But, while extracting a tooth might provide short-term belief, you may find yourself regretting the long-term consequences. Here’s why:

  1. A missing tooth is like the first in a row of dominoes. When it falls, the rest do so as well. That’s because your teeth rely on each other to stay strong and healthy.
  2. An extracted tooth leaves behind an empty socket that remains vulnerable to infection and damage throughout your life. Many patients opt for an extraction only to need the services of an emergency dentist in Texarkana soon after.
  3. A missing tooth will do more than affect your appearance. It can cause problems with eating, speaking and performing other important activities.

A root canal will safeguard your oral health and save you from pain and suffering. Make an appointment today to talk to your dentist about this safe, modern form of dental therapy.
