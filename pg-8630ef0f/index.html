How Do I Get Emergency Dental Care?

Our dental team can offer same-day appointments for patients who are experiencing urgent dental issues that require immediate attention. Examples may include a tooth that has been knocked out of the mouth or various kinds of tooth pain. If you think you’re experiencing a dental emergency, give our dental office a call and explain your symptoms. Not only will we schedule an appointment for you, but we will also give you advice for managing your discomfort and protecting your mouth from further harm.

If your life is in danger, you should call 911. Oral health issues that might put your life at risk include a fractured or dislocated jaw, swelling that interferes with breathing or swallowing, and bleeding in the mouth that doesn’t stop even after pressure has been applied.

How Do I Find the Best Place to Get Dental Work Done?

Oftentimes, it can help to clearly define the traits that you want your new dentist to have and then search for practices that meet the specified criteria. Many patients want a dentist who is willing to accept their dental insurance plan, is in a location that is easy to get to, and offers a wide range of services. It always helps to get the perspective of someone else who has visited the practice, so check Google, Facebook, and Healthgrades for any reviews that past patients may have left.

What Do You Do If You Can’t Afford a Dentist?

We try to do what we can to make paying for your dental care easier. We gladly file on behalf of all dental PPO providers, and we’re in-network with Texas BlueCross BlueShield, Connection Dental, and Cigna. On top of that, we accept CareCredit financing, which can be a good option for people who would prefer to break up the cost of their care into smaller monthly installments instead of needing to pay everything all at once.
