Achieve Your Dream Smile with Texarkana Cosmetic Dentist

Call (430) 342-1083Request appointment

Your teeth are well cared for and you visit the dentist for your regular cleaning and checkup every six months, but you’re still unhappy with your smile. You’re not alone. As many as half of your peers feel the same way. You don’t have to let flaws make you smile closed-lip. Our skilled dentists offer many cosmetic dentistry procedures to brighten your smile.

More and more patients are learning about the benefits of cosmetic dentistry procedures. Not only do patients enjoy a new look, they also feel the renewed sense of confidence that comes with an attractive smile. With so many advancements in dentistry, many procedures require minimal visits to achieve the maximum result.

Teeth Whitening

Over time, teeth begin to yellow and our lifestyle choices can add to discoloration. Food, drinks, and tobacco can all take your teeth from pearly white to an unsightly dull shade. Revitalizing your smile might be as simple as teeth whitening. It’s safe, effective, and requires no anesthesia. Our dentists can professionally whiten your teeth in our Texarkana office with Zoom! whitening, or we can create custom whitening trays for home use. Our in-office Zoom! whitening treatment takes about an hour, and can brighten smiles up to ten shades. Our take-home whitening kits offer the same dramatic results over the course of two weeks of daily application. This is a great option for those patients who have sensitive teeth or who prefer to whiten their smiles gradually.

Cosmetic Bonding

Bonding is a technique used to restore a chipped, slightly misaligned, or otherwise imperfect tooth. In one visit, our dentists use a putty-like composite resin that is shaded to match your teeth. Hardened with a curing light, the resin is then shaped into a more pleasing form, perfecting your smile.

An inlay or onlay can be a good option for replacing a large filling without having to place a full crown. Our dentists take a conservative treatment approach, making sure that patients retain as much natural tooth structure as possible. Inlays and onlays fit into the tooth similar to a filling allowing for a beautiful, natural smile.

Porcelain Veneers

Porcelain veneers are one of the most popular cosmetic dentistry procedures today. Veneers are made from layers of thin porcelain sheaths, and once placed, they completely cover the front of your teeth. Our dentists can completely transform the look of your smile with porcelain veneers making gaps disappear, misshapen teeth well-formed, and chipped teeth whole. Leaving you with a stunning smile makeover. A quick consultation in our office is all you need to get started with the right option for you.

From regular preventive care to elective cosmetic dentistry, a beautiful smile is just a call away. With a history of excellence serving patients from Texarkana and surrounding areas, our dentists invite you to make an appointment and discover your smile’s full potential. Call our Texarkana dental office today to schedule your personal consultation.

Which Cosmetic Dental Service Is Right for Me?

There are various cosmetic services available to meet a wide range of needs. Here are some of the popular services that we offer.

  • Cosmetic Bonding: This is a quick, non-invasive procedure that is ideal for making smaller changes. This includes repairing chipped teeth and small gaps. It is the most affordable option but only lasts 3 to 10 years before needing to be replaced.
  • Teeth Whitening: Professional teeth whitening can be completed quickly, and it is more effective than any whitening treatment that you can find over the counter.
  • Veneers: If you want to change multiple aspects of your smile at the same time, veneers are the easiest way to achieve this. They are more of an investment, but can correct a wide range of imperfections, like stains, chips, undersized teeth, misshapen teeth, and more!

Cosmetic Dentistry Can Be an Investment Worth Making

Cosmetic dentistry may seem expensive at first, but you need to consider the investment that you are making in your smile. To find out if a treatment is right for you, think about the outcome and how it affects you. If you aren’t confident with your smile now, cosmetic dentistry may be able to make the changes you desire. An increased sense of confidence means better relationships, an improved social life, and more professional achievements.

How to Make Cosmetic Dentistry Affordable

Cosmetic dentistry isn’t usually covered by dental insurance because it is considered to be elective. However, this doesn’t mean that you are completely on your own. We are happy to accept patients through CareCredit – a third-party financing company that can split the cost of your treatment into manageable monthly installments with little to no interest.

Cosmetic Dentistry FAQs

Is Cosmetic Dentistry Covered by Insurance?

Unfortunately, most dental insurance plans do not cover elective procedures, and most cosmetic dentistry treatments are considered elective. However, there still may be exceptions, especially if the treatment also provides oral health benefits, such as if a veneer builds up or protects a damaged tooth.

Typically, dental insurance will cover approximately 50 to 80 percent of the cost in these cases; however, this will depend on your individual policy. The team at Pleasant Grove Family Dentistry can help you understand your specific plan, file your paperwork, and even maximize your benefits.

How Long Does Cosmetic Dentistry Last?

The lifespan of your cosmetic dentistry procedure depends on which treatments you’ve had, as some last longer than others. Faster, easier treatments like professional teeth whitening and cosmetic bonding often do not last as long as other treatments like porcelain veneers do.

A general rule of thumb is the better you take care of your teeth and gums, the longer you can expect your results to last. This means brushing twice a day, flossing once a day, visiting Pleasant Grove Family Dentistry for your regular biannual cleanings and exams, wearing a mouthguard or nightguard, not using your teeth as tools, and avoiding smoking and foods and beverages that could cause stains.

Does Cosmetic Dentistry Hurt?

Many patients hold off on cosmetic dentistry out of a fear of pain. Thankfully, your dentist understands this, and will work with you to ensure that your treatment is as comfortable as possible.

Most cosmetic dentistry treatments are non or minimally invasive. In fact, professional whitening and cosmetic bonding don’t even require a local anesthetic.

For porcelain veneers, your dentist needs to reshape your teeth, but your mouth will be numbed before this step. Following the procedure, you may experience some mild soreness and sensitivity for the next few days, however this is typically well managed with the help of over-the-counter pain relievers.

How Do I Know If I Need Cosmetic Dentistry?

While cosmetic dentistry is not necessarily a “need,” if you’re not 100 percent satisfied with the appearance of your smile, you may benefit from one of the many available cosmetic dentistry treatments.

Stained or discolored teeth can be difficult to correct with over-the-counter products, but with professional whitening from your dentist, you can see noticeable changes in about an hour!

If you have chipped or cracked teeth, this can not only put you at risk for cavities and infection, but it can also hurt your confidence. Cosmetic bonding can quickly and easily restore the appearance of your broken tooth in a way that’s both beautiful and durable.

Finally, if you’re concerned about your smile adding years to your appearance, cosmetic dentistry can help. Whiter, more evenly spaced teeth can take years off your appearance and help boost your self-esteem in the process.

Ultimately, the best way to determine if cosmetic dentistry is right for you is to visit Pleasant Grove Family Dentistry for a consultation. Your dentist can review your health and provide you with a treatment plan that will address your issues and help you achieve your smile goals.
