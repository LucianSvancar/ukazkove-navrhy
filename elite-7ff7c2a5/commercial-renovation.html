Commercial Renovation & Construction

From remodeling a small office to building a multi-story business from the ground up, Elite Contractors can handle every phase of your project and fulfill all of your construction needs. No matter how big or small, we will complete your project on time and within your budget.

Team Elite has completed full-service construction projects for small businesses, apartment complexes, corporations, schools, non-profit organizations, and more in Jackson and throughout Tennessee and the Southeast US. We invest considerable time and energy into understanding your business and how it works; that way, we can plan your future growth and map out a better strategic plan today.

Take a look at some of our past commercial construction projects and reach out to get started!

Commercial﹣New Construction

ESC Wilger

12,000 SF new construction build of production facility that manufactures farm equipment spray nozzles. All construction trades required for this build performed under the supervision of Elite Contractors.

Project details
  • Monolithic concrete slab
  • Clear span metal framed building
  • R paneled metal roof installed
  • Flute filled Duro-Last white membrane roof installed over R paneled roof for additional cooling effect
  • 3 phase commercial wiring with high bay LED lighting
  • Spray foam insulation
  • Plumbing & plumbing fixtures for a new break room
  • Fire suppression including sprinkler heads & enunciators
  • High bay overhead garage doors
  • Drywall & paint
  • Also renovated an existing 12,000 SF existing building with construction trades including electrical (high bay LED lighting), fire suppression, commercial storefront replacement, and roofing installation (Duro-Last white membrane).
