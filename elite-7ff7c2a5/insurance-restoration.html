Swift Insurance Restoration Solutions

Let Us Handle Your Insurance Claim and Restoration Project with Confidence

Insurance Restoration

Fire, wind, hail, or water damage to your home or commercial property can happen at a moment’s notice. Let Elite Contractors serve as your mitigation contractor. Our expertise ensures you’ll receive the insurance claims settlement you deserve in a timely and efficient manner.

Our approach to claims management is systematic and effective. We write our insurance claims on the same software, Xactimate, which all major insurance companies use. By using Xactimate, Elite Contractors demonstrates an immediate level of professionalism to the adjuster, allowing us to work more efficiently and effectively for our clients.

No matter if you’re a residential or commercial client, one call to us will give you the peace of mind of knowing real professionals are managing your claim, as well as your construction project, from start to completion.
