About us

Family-run roofing and construction in Bismarck, AR, serving central Arkansas.

A family-operated business with over 26 years in roofing

JJ & SON’S Roofing And Construction is a family-operated business with over 26 years of experience in the roofing industry. Our central location in Bismarck, AR, allows us to serve all of central Arkansas. We take pride in treating our customers like family, and our goal is to provide quality and affordable roofing solutions that protect your greatest investment.

Shingles, metal and every type of flat roof

Our team of licensed, bonded, and insured professionals is equipped to handle various roofing projects. We approach each roof as if it were our own, with meticulous attention to detail and exceptional results. You get a free quote tailored to your specific needs, from a roofing company that puts your satisfaction first.

As part of our commitment to excellence, we have established partnerships with leading suppliers such as ABC Supply and Metal Roofing Supply.

Quality craftsmanship and reliable solutions

We know the roof above your house or place of business is more than simply a physical structure. We are dedicated to providing top-notch craftsmanship and dependable solutions that last. We carefully choose superior components from dependable vendors to ensure the toughness and endurance of your roof.

Timely and efficient roofing solutions

We understand how a roofing project can interfere with your everyday activities, so we work hard to complete each project as quickly and effectively as possible. Our devoted crew stays on schedule without sacrificing quality, and your project is carried out efficiently from beginning to end, reducing inconvenience.

Personalized service tailored to your needs

We are firm believers in the value of individualized care. When you work with us you can expect a customized strategy that takes into account your particular roofing needs. Our helpful and experienced staff takes the time to learn about your objectives and worries before offering frank advice and open communication throughout the entire process.

Your satisfaction is our priority

Your happiness is the inspiration for everything we do. Until you are completely satisfied with the results, we are not satisfied. Call us for a free consultation and estimate, and together we can make sure your roofing needs are addressed skillfully and carefully.
