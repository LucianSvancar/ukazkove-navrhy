Frequently asked questions

Roofing, siding, concrete and storm damage questions, answered.

How do I know if my roof needs replacing?

Missing shingles, granule loss, sagging sections, and water stains on interior ceilings are common signs. Arkansas's storm seasons accelerate wear, so annual inspections help catch damage before it becomes a costly replacement.

What roofing materials work best in Arkansas?

Architectural asphalt shingles are the most popular choice for Arkansas homes due to their durability against heat, humidity, and hail. Impact-resistant options provide added protection in areas prone to severe storms and high winds.

How does commercial roofing differ from residential?

Commercial roofs are typically flat or low-slope and use systems like TPO or modified bitumen rather than shingles. They require specialized installation techniques and different drainage considerations than standard residential pitched roofs.

How often should a commercial roof be inspected?

Twice a year is the general recommendation — once in spring and once in fall. Regular inspections catch membrane splits, ponding water, and flashing failures early, significantly extending the lifespan of a commercial roof.

What siding options hold up best in humid climates?

Fiber cement and vinyl siding both perform well in Arkansas's humid conditions. Fiber cement resists moisture, rot, and insects, while vinyl requires minimal upkeep and won't warp or fade under prolonged sun and humidity exposure.

Can new siding improve my home's energy efficiency?

Yes, insulated siding adds a thermal barrier that reduces heat transfer through exterior walls. In Arkansas summers, this can noticeably lower cooling costs while also reducing outside noise and improving overall curb appeal.

What concrete flatwork services are available for homeowners?

Driveways, patios, sidewalks, and garage floors are the most common residential concrete projects. Proper site prep and mix selection are critical in Arkansas, where freeze-thaw cycles and clay-heavy soil can crack poorly installed slabs.

How long does a concrete driveway typically last?

A properly installed concrete driveway can last 30 years or more with routine maintenance. Sealing every few years and promptly addressing cracks helps prevent water infiltration that accelerates deterioration, especially during Arkansas winters.

When is the best time to schedule roofing work in Arkansas?

Late spring and early fall offer the most favorable installation conditions, avoiding peak summer heat and winter cold snaps. Scheduling outside tornado season also reduces the risk of weather delays disrupting your project timeline.

Why does siding installation require professional expertise?

Improper overlap, gaps around trim, or missed moisture barriers can allow water intrusion that causes hidden structural damage. Professional installers ensure correct flashing, sealing, and fastening patterns that protect your home long-term.

What are the signs that my gutters need repair or replacement?

Gutters likely need attention when you notice sagging sections, visible cracks or rust, water spilling over the sides during rain, or pooling around your foundation. In Arkansas, where heavy storm seasons put gutters under repeated stress, catching these warning signs early can prevent costly water damage to your roof and home exterior.
