After hail or wind

After a storm: what homeowners can do

General guidance for Oklahoma weather. It is not a statement about services offered by Mackey Roofing and not insurance or legal advice.

  1. 1

    Stay safe, look from the ground

    Check for missing or lifted shingles, dents and debris from the ground or with binoculars. Do not climb on a damaged roof.

  2. 2

    Document it

    Photograph the damage and note the date of the storm before anything is moved or covered.

  3. 3

    Call your insurer early

    Ask how claims work and what your deductible is. Keep receipts for any emergency repairs.

  4. 4

    Get it inspected

    Have a roofer look at the roof before permanent repairs. If it leaks, a temporary tarp can limit damage meanwhile.
