Before you book

What to expect from a roof replacement

A general overview for homeowners, not a description of Mackey Roofing’s own process. Ask the team how they work when you call.

  1. 1

    Inspection

    A roofer looks over the roof surface, decking and flashing and explains what they find, including photos or a walk-through if you ask.

  2. 2

    Written quote

    You get the scope and price in writing before work starts. Ask what is included: tear-off, underlayment, flashing, clean-up, and the shingle or metal product.

  3. 3

    Installation

    Old roofing comes off, any damaged decking is repaired, new roofing goes on and the site is cleaned up. Timing depends on roof size and weather.
