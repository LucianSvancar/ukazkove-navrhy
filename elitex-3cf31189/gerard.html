Gerard roofing

Stone-coated steel roofing with a lifetime transferable warranty.

Elite is a top preferred installer of all Gerard roofing systems. Gerard is one of the stone-coated steel roofing lines we install: industrial strength steel panels covered with tiny stone chips, combining the look of traditional stone or wood with the durability of metal.

Durable roofing

All but immune to rain, hail and wind, and rated to withstand fire and earthquake damage. Many insurance companies give a discount for having it installed.

Cost effective roofing

Gerard has a higher initial cost, but it makes up the difference over time. The average lifespan of an asphalt shingle roof is around 15 years, while a Gerard roof has a lifetime, transferable warranty.

Many options to choose from

An extremely wide variety of styles and colors replicating wood shake, granite shingles and clay tiles, stronger and much lighter than the originals.
