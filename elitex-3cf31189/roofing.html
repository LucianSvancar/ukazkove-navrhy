Experience and integrity

A majority of roof problems start with improper insulation and poor quality materials. We are here when you need us, whether to inspect your roof after a big storm or to provide annual roofing maintenance.

Signs your roof needs repair or maintenance

  • Sagging. A surefire sign that water has damaged the underlying wood. Usually a portion of the roof needs to be replaced.
  • Dark spots. Dark spots on the wood in your attic mean water has found its way in. Finding the source is the way to know what repairs are needed.
  • Missing shingles. Replace them as soon as possible, otherwise water can enter your home.
  • Mold. Another indicator that moisture has found its way inside. We repair water-damaged areas that have begun to grow mold.
  • Visible damage. Check your roof after a major storm, especially one with hail and high wind.
