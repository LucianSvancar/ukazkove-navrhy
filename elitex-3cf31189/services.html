Services

Roofing, siding, windows, gutters, construction, restoration and remodeling.

Our company

When it comes to your house, you need a company you trust no matter what the problem may be. Whether you need a roof inspection, windows installed or restoration work, Elite Exteriors Roofing & Restoration, Inc. is here for you.

As your roofing company, our goal is not to tell you why we are the best, but to show you what makes us stand out. We listen to your needs and get to know what you are looking to accomplish.

The work we do

From roofing to siding, gutters and windows, construction, restoration and remodeling, your home can be everything you have always dreamed it could be.

  • Roofing: Residential and commercial roofing: replacement, repair, inspections and annual maintenance.
  • Commercial roofing: Commercial roof maintenance, repair and replacement for Hot Springs businesses.
  • Residential roofing: Roof replacement, repair, storm damage, luxury and exotic roofing for homes.
  • Siding: Siding repair and replacement from name-brand suppliers.
  • Windows and gutters: Window installation, gutter cleaning and new gutters.
  • Construction and remodel: New construction, additions and remodeling, from first meeting to last nail.

Why choose us

No matter what you are looking for, we are confident we can help you. Your home is in good hands when you trust Elite Exteriors.
