Referral rewards

Referral reward levels from a $250 gift card to a trip for two to Hawaii.

Refer a customer, get rewarded

Bronze1 qualified customer

$250 gift card to one of our reward partners

Silver3 qualified customers

$500 gift card

Gold5 qualified customers

$1,000 gift card

Platinum10 qualified customers

Trip for two to Hawaii, with roundtrip airfare and hotel

How it works

  1. Submit your referral online. Fill out the form before your referral has any contact with Elite Exteriors.
  2. We reach out to them. A representative contacts your referral and arranges a roof inspection, with no obligation and no charge.
  3. Receive your reward. Once their roof is built and they have signed their Certificate of Completion, you receive the reward for your level.

Program details

  • A referral is a homeowner who contracts with Elite Exteriors for a new or replacement roof of at least 1,500 square feet. Insurance claims are welcome.
  • A referral becomes a qualified customer once their roof is built and they have signed their Certificate of Completion.
  • You may not refer your own primary residence. If we replaced your primary residence roof, you may refer a second home, rental property and so on.
  • Referrals may not also take part in our non-profit 501(c)3 referral program.
  • Qualified customers within 1 year (365 days) of your first submission count toward your level. After one year your level restarts.
  • The Hawaii trip is 5 days and 4 nights with roundtrip coach airfare for two and double occupancy hotel, in a hotel of Elite choice. No substitutions or modifications.
