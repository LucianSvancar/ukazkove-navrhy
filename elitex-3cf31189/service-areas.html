Service areas

Roofing and restoration in Arkansas, Tennessee, Mississippi and Louisiana.

Your roofing contractors across the Southeast

Since 1999, Elite Exteriors Roofing & Restoration has served Arkansas, and extends its services to states across the Southeast. We specialize in all types of roofing and offer repairs, replacements and installations.

Arkansas

Roof repair, roof replacement and new construction roof installation in Hot Springs, Little Rock, Benton, Hot Springs Village, Bryant and Conway. We also offer roof restoration services, which can extend the life of your roof. We are family-owned and operated and stand behind our work with a 100% satisfaction guarantee.

Tennessee, Mississippi and Louisiana

We also serve customers in Tennessee, Mississippi and Louisiana. If you need roofing or restoration services in Arkansas, Tennessee, Mississippi or Louisiana, give us a call.

What we offer in every area

  • Roofing. Repair and restoration for residential and commercial roofs from the nation leading roofing manufacturers.
  • Gutters. Repair, installation and replacement on homes and commercial buildings in a wide variety of colors.
  • Siding. Top quality siding repair and replacement with industry-leading warranties.
  • Windows. Custom, vinyl, composite and wood window installation from top manufacturers and certified installers.
  • Solar. Solar panel installation to help you save on your energy bill.
