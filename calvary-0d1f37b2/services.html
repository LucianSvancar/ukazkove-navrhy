Services

Plumbing problems we can talk through

Calvary Plumbing is a plumbing company serving Tyler and surrounding towns (Facebook). Below are the most common problems homeowners call about. Calvary has not confirmed an exact service list yet, so please ask when you call.

Leaks and burst pipes

Visible drips, wet spots on walls or ceilings, a water bill that suddenly jumps.

  • Shut off the water first
  • Describe where the water shows up
  • Photos help us understand it on the phone

Hot water problems

No hot water, water that runs lukewarm or runs out fast.

  • Gas or electric heater?
  • Age of the heater, if you know it
  • Any water around the tank

Drains and clogs

Slow sinks, gurgling, several drains backing up at once.

  • Which fixtures are affected
  • Plunger tried?
  • No chemical cleaners please

Toilets and fixtures

Running or leaking toilets, dripping faucets, loose fixtures.

  • Which fixture and room
  • How long it has been running
  • Stop valve closed?

Water pressure

Weak flow at one tap or across the whole house.

  • One tap or all taps
  • Sudden or gradual
  • Main valve fully open

Something else

Not sure what to call it? Describe it, we tell you if it is something we can help with.

  • Tell us the town
  • Tell us what you see or hear
  • We call you back

Service categories are common examples for a plumbing company and are not a confirmed price list or service list of Calvary Plumbing LLC (unverified).
