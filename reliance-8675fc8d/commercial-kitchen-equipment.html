Experienced commercial kitchen equipment professionals

We have made it our business to understand the unique service needs of a commercial kitchen, and are dedicated to fulfilling them with speed and excellence. We can provide cleaning, maintenance, parts, repair, and replacement for a large variety of equipment, and are familiar with all the latest technology and techniques related to a modern commercial kitchen.

Brand or model variety is no problem for the team at Reliance Mechanical Contractors. We train our technicians to provide service for all the major brands and models of equipment for cooking, specialty foodservice, washing, and refrigeration in a commercial kitchen.

Commercial kitchen equipment is expensive, and regular care by skilled professionals is important to getting the most out of it. A properly cared for appliance can last for years and add great value to a business, but a neglected one can be an expensive drain on resources.
