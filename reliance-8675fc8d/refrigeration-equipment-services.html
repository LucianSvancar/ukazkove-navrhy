Commercial refrigeration equipment is an expensive investment that most business owners want to protect, and that is where Reliance Mechanical Contractors is ready to help. We maintain an excellent team of refrigeration equipment service technicians, and have them ready to help you on a 24/7 basis. If your business is in the Ark-La-Tex area and you need commercial refrigeration equipment services, emergency or otherwise, call us.

Licensed and certified technicians

We hire experienced refrigeration professionals with a talent for repairing and maintaining commercial refrigeration equipment, and we require them to keep up with all the latest tools, technology, and training in the refrigeration industry.

Our team is experienced working with a wide range of equipment and a great variety of brands, from state of the art refrigeration equipment to equipment that has seen steady daily use for years. Some of the most common services ordered by our customers include emergency repair, preventative maintenance, and equipment inspections.

Equipment we service

  • Cold display cases
  • Beer or bottle coolers
  • Keg refrigerators
  • Walk-in refrigerators and freezers
  • Deep freezers
  • Ice machines
  • Cold prep tables and a variety of other refrigeration tools

Refrigeration equipment is essential to maintaining the quality of cold inventory, and when it goes out unexpectedly it can become an emergency situation. Our 24/7 licensed and certified technicians can be available in Texarkana and the surrounding areas at a moment’s notice. We service cities in Texas, Arkansas, and Louisiana: contact us to find out if you are in our service area.
