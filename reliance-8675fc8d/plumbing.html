Reliance Mechanical Contractors is a licensed & insured 24 hour plumbing company that provides water heater repairs, leak detection, repairs to stopped up drains, sewer line repair, and many other plumbing repairs. Reliance offers plumbers licensed in Arkansas, Louisiana, and Texas to provide plumbing service in Texarkana and the entire Ark-La-Tex area.

DIY plumbing repairs can quickly become an overwhelming experience. Reliance has been helping homeowners and businesses take care of difficult plumbing problems in Texarkana for over 30 years. Call Reliance Mechanical to schedule an appointment with one of our licensed plumbers today for expert advice on plumbing repairs, maintenance, or installation.

Our plumbing services include

  • Toilet repair
  • Leak detection and repair
  • Water heater repair and installation, traditional and tankless
  • Back-flow prevention and testing
  • Natural gas line testing, installation, and repair
  • Garbage disposal repair and installation
  • Sewer line repair and inspection
  • Toilet, sink, or fixture installation
  • Drain and sewer line pressure jetting
  • Water and sewer line location
  • Pipe installation or replacement
  • Water filtration and water softener installation
  • Other miscellaneous plumbing repair
