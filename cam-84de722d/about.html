About CAM Electric

Owned by Craig McManus. Union City, Tennessee.

CAM Electric is an electrical company in Union City, Tennessee, owned by Craig McManus. It does commercial and residential service, electrical wiring, control systems, lighting systems, backup generators and emergency power, neon and lighted signs, swimming pools and irrigation systems.

The company's website states that it is licensed and insured, and that the owner is also a licensed master electrician and electrical contractor in the State of Kentucky.
