About Our Company

Family owned and operated since 2013

Arkansas River Valley Heating and Air has been serving the Arkansas River Valley area since 2013 and is a family-owned and operated company. Owner Tony Resinger has more than 26 years of experience providing excellent heating and cooling services.

We pride ourselves on offering all of our clients great customer service and fantastic products at reasonable prices. Our cooling installation work is done quickly and efficiently with minimal disruption to the working environment. Our expert technicians will always provide friendly and convenient service tailored to your needs.

Our expert team will assist you in finding the perfect unit for your needs. We can recommend and install a unit that will suit any size room and provide you with efficient climate control, no matter what the weather.

Go green and shrink your bills

Energy efficiency is on everybody mind these days. It is not only important for the environment, it means cheaper energy bills for our customers. Our cooling installation team can advise you on what type of system will suit your needs and where you should place it.
