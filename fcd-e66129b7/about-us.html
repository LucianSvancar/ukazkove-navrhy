From your child’s first dental check-up through adolescence and into adulthood our procedures focus on comprehensive dental treatment including cosmetic dentistry and dental implants. Our services include routine dental examinations, dental cleanings, tooth extractions, root canal therapy, dental crowns, dental bridges, bonding, teeth whitening, and Invisalign® or orthodontic care.

We treat and restore your tooth function and smile following tooth loss. Dental implants provide tooth replacement options with a single implant, a bridge implant, and implant dentures.

Your total or partial tooth loss can be treated with dentures. Full dentures or partial dentures are available to restore your tooth function and appearance when you’ve lost some or all of your teeth.

We provide you and your family a relaxing environment. Nitrous Oxide (laughing gas) is available to our patients upon request.

Most major dental benefit plans are accepted. And flexible payment solutions are available as well.

Contact FCD today to schedule for all of your family dental needs.
