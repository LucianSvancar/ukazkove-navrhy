Tooth replacement options are available at our Jackson dental office if you’ve lost one tooth or multiple teeth. Ask us about dental implants.

Single implant

A strong replacement when you’ve lost one tooth. The gap caused by tooth loss can cause problems if you delay tooth replacement. Single implants fill and restore your missing tooth gap.

Your bone and gum tissue are at risk when you have a missing tooth. The remaining gap can provide an opportunity for your surrounding teeth to move and become misaligned.

A single implant creates a new tooth root. Your implant is made of a surgical grade of titanium and is placed via oral surgery into your bone and gum tissue.

After a necessary healing period your new tooth (a dental crown) is secured to the upper portion of your implant. Your tooth secured by a single implant can last for over 10 years with proper care.

Contact our Jackson dental office about tooth replacement with single implant. Schedule an oral examination to discuss your tooth loss and receive more information about dental implants.

Bridge implant

Security for your tooth replacement. Bridge implants use the security of dental implants to support your new teeth. Implants provide a stable foundation for your tooth restoration.

Your treatment with bridge implants relies on healthy gum and bone tissue to support each implant that’s placed. Single implants are placed at specific locations in your jawbone where they form a new tooth root and adapt to your tissue.

Bridge implants are not directly vulnerable to decay. They will provide lasting tooth restoration with proper dental care.

Contact your Jackson dentist about your missing teeth. Schedule an examination to discuss your tooth replacement options including bridge implants.

Implant dentures

Stabilize your denture. An implant denture provides a strong, secure replacement when you’ve lost all or most of your teeth. Unlike standard dentures, your implant denture will not shift or move.

Implant dentures are anchored to your jawbone. Dental implants form secure points of attachment for your denture in your upper and/or lower jaw.

You can remove your implant denture. This promotes easier cleaning and the option to observe your gum and bone tissue beneath them.

Contact our Jackson dental office about your missing teeth and/or your current denture. Schedule an examination to discuss the secure benefit of an implant denture.
