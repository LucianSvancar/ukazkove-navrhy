Professional teeth cleaning

with the Air-Flow Master Piezon ®

Dental hygiene is part of everyday healthcare. Basic dental care consists of using a standard toothbrush and an appropriate toothpaste, interdental brushes, and finally an appropriate mouthwash. Even with this care performed daily, dental plaque or pigmentation may build up, especially in hard-to-access spots. These are best removed by a dentist in a short treatment using ultrasound or airflow devices.

Our practice is equipped with a modern Air-Flow Master Piezon® device, which combines ultrasound with mechanical abrasion. This device can remove dental calculus, plaque, and pigmentation and polish the teeth. We can attest from personal experience that treatment with the Air-Flow Master Piezon® device is quite painless.

[IMG /domains/0001/userfiles/images/aiflowmasterpiezon.jpg alt=] Ultrasound component

This function of the device is mainly useful in removing dental calculus. The ultrasound component of the device is outfitted with an LED light source using cutting-edge Piezon NO PAIN technology, which provides a powerful yet effectively PAINLESS treatment.

Abrasion component – Air-Flow

This part of the device provides a gentle way to rid your teeth of pigmentation (discoloration). The procedure cleans and removes pigmentation from the interdental spaces, as well as from the smooth surfaces of the teeth that are difficult to clean at home. After treatment your teeth will be clean, smooth, and naturally white.

This method is sometimes called “dental peeling.“ Peeling is not just for the skin!
